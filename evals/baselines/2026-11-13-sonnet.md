## What it shows

- **With the skill every run passed**, 48 of 48. Without it, 47 of 48: one
  run of the backtest (22) drew its trades' entries and exits and shaded no
  zone between them.
- **With the skill, a task takes two thirds of the turns**: 10 against 15,
  169k tokens against 221k (−24%), $0.12 against $0.14.
- **The compiled code is hardly read any more**: in 1 run of 48 with the
  skill and 6 of 48 without, where it was 2 and 22 on 1.48.0. The types and
  their doc comments answer what the code was opened for: without the skill
  34 runs of 48 read the types, 11 the README.
- **Without the skill a task got cheaper**: 15 turns against 16, 221k tokens
  against 240k on 1.48.0.
- **With the skill a task costs more tokens than it did**: 169k against 137k
  on 1.48.0, at the same 10 turns. Why was not looked into; the skill is
  longer than it was then.
- **One task is slower with the skill**: the depth ladder (18: 22 turns
  against 17). Replay (13) and the React component (21) are level.
- At two runs a task, a few turns either way is noise; a change of half is
  not.
