## What it shows

- **Every run passed**, 96 of 96. The two failures of the baseline (tasks
  18 and 20, with the skill) passed both runs.
- **With the skill, a task took less**: 10 turns against 12, 137k tokens
  against 177k (−23%), $0.10 against $0.11. Without it, about as before:
  16 turns, 240k tokens against 251k, $0.15.
- **The two tasks the skill was slowest at came down by half**: price
  alerts on a price fed by hand (07: 22 turns → 11) and a Vietnamese stock
  (10: 21 → 12). Both were findings 2 and 5.
- **With the skill, the compiled code was read in 2 runs of 48** (12 in the
  baseline), both on task 09: the chart's style keys (finding 13).
  Without it, 22 of 48 (25 before): what for is in findings 11, 12 and 14.
- **One task got slower with the skill**: the chart's look (09: 11 turns →
  16). Both runs searched the packages for the candles' and wicks' style
  keys; the skill names two keys and the object they are in, not where it
  is or the wicks' keys (finding 13).
- At two runs a task, a few turns either way is noise; a change of half is
  not.
