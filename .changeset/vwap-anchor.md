**The built-in VWAP starts again each trading day.** This changes the line you see. Until now `vwap` ran over every bar loaded and never reset, so its value depended on how much history was on the chart and moved when older bars were paged in. It now has an `anchor` input: `'session'`, the default, starts again each trading day, from midnight in the exchange's time zone (the symbol's `timeZone`, else the market's from `setMarket`, else UTC); `'week'` each Monday; `'month'` on the 1st; `'all'` over every bar loaded, as it was. An anchor it does not know is read as `'session'`. Every value after the first loaded day changes, and the line breaks at each new day. Daily charts change most: there a session's VWAP is each bar's typical price, drawn as one line, so choose `'week'` or `'month'` on daily or weekly charts. Weeks start on Monday at midnight, so the Sunday-evening open of FX and futures counts in the week before, and a session that runs past midnight (futures open 17:00 to 16:00) is split there.

A VWAP saved by 1.51 or earlier in a layout, a template or a saved default comes back with the session anchor; `chart.updateIndicator(id, { anchor: 'all' })` keeps the old line. A layout saved by 1.51 or earlier applied to a chart that already shows that VWAP keeps the anchor it has. Alerts on a VWAP line see it jump at each reset, so a "price crosses VWAP" alert can fire at a session's open. In TypeScript, `addIndicator('vwap', ...)` now takes only `anchor`; any other key stops compiling. The chip, the legend and the CSV column are still named `VWAP`.

A live tick or a new bar is added to the sums kept for the bar before it, so no bar of the period is read again: on 100,000 minute bars a tick took about 2 µs with `'session'`, `'week'` or `'month'`, where a full computation takes 4 to 5 ms, as `vwap` did on every frame. `'all'` still computes in full.

Indicators get the exchange's zone as `config.exchangeTimeZone`, set by the chart from `setSymbolInfo` and `setMarket` (`IndicatorEngine.setExchangeTimeZone(zone)` for an engine of your own), never saved in a layout; the zone the chart shows (`setTimeZone`) does not move any values. A symbol whose info brings a new zone computes its indicators once, and a symbol in UTC, as every crypto feed names it, sets no zone, so a switch between two of them computes nothing again. While a connected chart asks its feed about the symbol it switches to, the indicators keep the last symbol's zone: a symbol on the same exchange then computes them once, when its bars come, one elsewhere again when its info comes, and a feed that has nothing to say about it, or says nothing within 5 s (as long as the days a feed's wrapper builds wait for it, `SYMBOL_ZONE_WAIT_MS` in `@tradecanvas/core`), brings back the market's zone, else UTC. A switch made of `disconnectStream()` and a `connect()` right after it, as ChartWidget's symbol and interval switches are, holds the zone the same way. The bars the chart builds from trades (`aggregateTicks`) keep it as long, so on the same exchange the next symbol's trades go on building the day its history ends on. `setSymbolInfo(null)` drops the zone at once. Session VWAP (`svwap`), the MTF moving average (`mtfma`), the prior-day lines (`setPeriodLevelsVisible`) and the market profile's sessions now count their days in that zone too, so the chart has one definition of the day, and all of them read bar times given in seconds (before, `svwap`, `mtfma`, the prior-day lines and the market profile's split into days never started a new day on such bars). `mtfma`'s 2h to 12h frames (`timeframe: '4h'`…) start at the exchange's midnight too: in Ho Chi Minh City a 4h frame steps at 01:00, 05:00… UTC. A feed's own 4h bars stay on UTC's grid (00:00, 04:00…), so in a zone whose midnight falls between UTC's 4-hour marks (Ho Chi Minh City, Tokyo, New York in winter) a 4h MTF line steps at other hours than such a feed's 4h candles. They stay the same for UTC symbols, Vietnamese intraday hours and US regular hours, and change where a session crosses UTC midnight: the ASX and the NZX, US post-market bars between 19:00 and 20:00 in New York in winter, and overnight futures. On daily bars `svwap` now draws its line through each bar's typical price, where it drew nothing; its bands, which a session of one bar holds on that line, are left out, and so are their values: on daily bars its legend and the data window show the line alone, and the export leaves the band columns empty. Counting days in a zone costs a comparison a bar, not a look-up of the zone's offset. `computePeriodLevels` and `computeSessionProfiles` take the zone as a last argument and read one the browser does not know as UTC, and a session's `startTime` is its date on the exchange's clock, at 00:00 UTC.

Anchored VWAP (`avwap`) finds its anchor bar when the bars are timed in seconds: an `anchorTime` in milliseconds matched no bar and the line was empty. `anchorTime` may be in seconds too.
