**The built-in VWAP starts again each trading day.** This changes the line you see. Until now `vwap` ran over every bar loaded and never reset, so its value depended on how much history was on the chart and moved when older bars were paged in. It now has an `anchor` input: `'session'`, the default, starts again each trading day, from midnight in the exchange's time zone (the symbol's `timeZone`, else the market's from `setMarket`, else UTC); `'week'` each Monday; `'month'` on the 1st; `'all'` over every bar loaded, as it was. An anchor it does not know is read as `'session'`. Every value after the first loaded day changes, and the line breaks at each new day. Daily charts change most: there a session's VWAP is each bar's typical price, drawn as one line, so choose `'week'` or `'month'` on daily or weekly charts. Weeks start on Monday at midnight, so the Sunday-evening open of FX and futures counts in the week before, and a session that runs past midnight (futures open 17:00 to 16:00) is split there.

A VWAP saved by 1.50 in a layout, a template or a saved default comes back with the session anchor; `chart.updateIndicator(id, { anchor: 'all' })` keeps the old line. A 1.50 layout applied to a chart that already shows that VWAP keeps the anchor it has. Alerts on a VWAP line see it jump at each reset, so a "price crosses VWAP" alert can fire at a session's open. In TypeScript, `addIndicator('vwap', ...)` now takes only `anchor`; any other key stops compiling. The chip, the legend and the CSV column are still named `VWAP`.

A live tick or a new bar sums the current period again, not the whole history: on 50,000 hourly bars a tick took 1 to 20 µs with `'session'`, `'week'` or `'month'`, where a full computation takes 2 to 5 ms, as `vwap` did on every frame. `'all'` still computes in full.

Indicators get the exchange's zone as `config.exchangeTimeZone`, set by the chart from `setSymbolInfo` and `setMarket` (`IndicatorEngine.setExchangeTimeZone(zone)` for an engine of your own), never saved in a layout; the zone the chart shows (`setTimeZone`) does not move any values. A symbol whose info brings a new zone computes its indicators once. Session VWAP (`svwap`), the MTF moving average (`mtfma`), the prior-day lines (`setPeriodLevelsVisible`) and the market profile's sessions now count their days in that zone too, so the chart has one definition of the day, and all of them read bar times given in seconds (before, `svwap`, `mtfma` and the prior-day lines never started a new day on such bars). They stay the same for UTC symbols, Vietnamese intraday hours and US regular hours, and change where a session crosses UTC midnight: the ASX and the NZX, US post-market bars between 19:00 and 20:00 in New York in winter, and overnight futures. On daily bars `svwap` now draws its line and bands through each bar's typical price; it drew nothing. `computePeriodLevels` and `computeSessionProfiles` take the zone as a last argument, and a session's `startTime` is its date on the exchange's clock, at 00:00 UTC.

Anchored VWAP (`avwap`) finds its anchor bar when the bars are timed in seconds: an `anchorTime` in milliseconds matched no bar and the line was empty. `anchorTime` may be in seconds too.
