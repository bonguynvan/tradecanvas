In the Graphite look the accent no longer fills a button or marks what is picked: the button that sends, a tick box, a radio and a slider's thumb are the text's colour, and what is on is on the picked ground. On the price axis an order's and a position's tags are outlined where a theme outlines the values on its axis (`Theme.valueTagsOutlined`), and in every theme are filled a shade deeper where their figure would not read.
