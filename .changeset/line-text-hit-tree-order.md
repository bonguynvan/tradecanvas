A click on the words on a trend line, ray, extended, info or angle line, arrow, horizontal or vertical line, or inside a rectangle, picks the drawing, and the toolbar over a selected line keeps clear of its words. Escape while a drawing is dragged calls the drag off: a moved or reshaped drawing goes back, a copy being dragged out goes, and nothing is left to undo. The widget's object tree lists the drawings as they stack, the one on top first, so the arrow keys on a grip move a drawing up the list and up the stack together.
