A Long/Short Position drawing can be placed as an order: selected in the widget with trading on, its toolbar shows **Create limit order**, which opens the order ticket filled from the drawing: its side, entry as the price, stop-loss, take-profit and the quantity its risk buys, taken down to the decimals the drawing writes. The order type follows the entry against the market (a buy limit below it, a stop above, the market at it), and nothing is sent until Place.
