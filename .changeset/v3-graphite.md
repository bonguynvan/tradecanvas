The Graphite look, and the widget's default: neutral grounds in dark and light, one corner of 3 px, controls on five heights, what is picked on a lighter ground with amber kept for what you act on. `GRAPHITE_DARK_THEME` and `GRAPHITE_LIGHT_THEME` are exported. `ui: 'modern'` keeps the look before it. The order ticket's button names the order ("Buy 100 HPG") with the order's value over it, and a position's Reverse and Close buttons come right after its P&L.
