The widget on a phone: below 641 px wide (its own width) the toolbar keeps the symbol, interval, chart type and indicators and puts the rest under a "more" button; a toolbar that doesn't fit at any width does the same. The drawing tools open from a button over the chart. On a phone, menus open as sheets from the bottom. A narrow grid shows one chart at a time with a tab for each. Menus close on a tap on the chart, and a chart with no size no longer pages through its feed's whole history.
