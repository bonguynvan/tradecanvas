Two style keys, `legend.upColor` and `legend.downColor`, colour a rise and a fall where a bar's values are read: the legend's close and change, the crosshair and pinned tooltips and the measure tool. Unset, they follow the main series' up and down, as before. A chart that hides its candles by making them transparent (an overlay drawing its own) sets these to keep the readouts visible.
