The widget no longer turns on the OpenType features `'cv11', 'ss01', 'ss03'` for its text. They were Inter's (a one-storey a, open digits, round quotes), left from when the widget drew in Inter, and they reached whatever face it draws in now, where a tag can mean something else: in IBM Plex, the Terminal look's face, they gave a one-storey a and slashed zeros. The widget's text is now drawn as its faces are. To turn on your face's own features, set `--tcw-font-features` on `.tcw-root` from your CSS (`--tcw-font-features: 'cv11', 'ss01'` for Inter), with or without the `ui` option. A `.tcw-root { font-feature-settings: normal }` of yours keeps working. The chart's canvases no longer take OpenType features from the page either (in Chromium a canvas's text did), so the axes and tags follow only the chart's font; the widget's tabular figures still reach them. The price axis is now measured on the chart's own canvas, where its labels are drawn: in the airy look, or with a proportional face, it was measured too narrow and cut off the end of the crosshair's price. In the airy look, a `chartOptions.font` that sets only sizes keeps the look's face on the axes; only a `family` there replaces it. The docs no longer say that `ChartOptions.font` and `setFont` size the legend: the legend takes the face at its own size (`setLegend({ fontSize })`), and in the widget its face and size come from `ui.font`.
