The widget, part by part. 112 feature switches, every one on until turned off and changeable while the widget runs: `features: { 'toolbar.replay': false, hotkeys: false }`, `widget.setFeatures(patch)`, `getFeatures()`, `isFeatureOn(name)` and `ChartWidgetGrid.setFeatures(patch)`. A name without a dot is a whole capability, off wherever it shows (its button, its menu entries, its command, its keys); a dotted name is one place: a toolbar button, a section of drawing tools, a status bar item, a menu entry, a key. The names are listed in `WIDGET_FEATURES`. The older on/off options (`toolbar`, `drawingTools`, `statusBar`, `rangeBar`, `alerts`…) are the same switches; the parts they turned off are now built and hidden, so they can be switched on later. Your own parts: `addToolbarDropdown` (a menu button), `addSidebarButton`, `addStatusBarItem`, `getSlot(name)` (beside the toolbar's controls, under the sidebar's, on the status bar, over the chart), and entries at the end of a drawing's menu (`drawingMenuItems`) and an indicator's (`indicatorMenuItems`). `addToolbarButton` now works with the toolbar switched off: the button shows with it (it used to return `null`). A Customization page in the docs gathers every way to customise.
