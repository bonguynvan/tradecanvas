**Copy chart image** works on a page whose Content-Security-Policy leaves `data:` out of `connect-src`. The image went to the clipboard by fetching a `data:` URL of itself, which such a policy refuses, so `chart.copyScreenshot()` gave `false` and the widget said "Copy failed — image clipboard unavailable" though the clipboard was fine. The canvas now encodes the PNG itself (`canvas.toBlob`), so copying needs nothing in `connect-src`; `chart.screenshotBlob()` and `Screenshot.toBlob()` no longer fetch either, and give `null` when there is nothing to encode. `copyScreenshot()` also hands the clipboard its item while the click or key that asked for it is still being handled, with the image still encoding, as Safari asks; a browser whose `ClipboardItem` takes only a finished image gets it once it is encoded. The widget's own needs are unchanged: `style-src 'unsafe-inline'` for the `<style>` element it adds, and `img-src data:` for the icons in its fields and checkboxes. The headless chart needs neither: the pinned tooltip (Alt+click a bar) wrote its move's percentage as markup with a `style` attribute, which a `style-src` without `'unsafe-inline'` refuses, so the percentage lost its fade and the page reported a violation. It is now built as elements, and shows a `priceFormatter`'s text as written, where an apostrophe or an `&` read `&#39;` or `&#38;`.
