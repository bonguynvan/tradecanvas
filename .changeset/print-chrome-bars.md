The widget's bars, rearranged. The interval menu lists each unit under its heading; the chart type shows as its icon; Compare sits beside Indicators when the widget has a data adapter; undo and redo move to the toolbar, and show where the drawing tools are off too (`toolbar.undo` and `toolbar.redo` take them away); the brackets, order ticket, depth ladder and account panel are one Trade menu. The drawing tools' menus name each tool's shortcut and have a star to pin it, and lock all and hide all join clear all. The status bar has a clock in the display time zone that opens the zones, and %, log and auto switches for the price scale; it no longer repeats the symbol. The toolbar over a selected drawing has a grip to drag it out of the way. New switches: `toolbar.compare`, `toolbar.undo`, `toolbar.redo`, `toolbar.trade`, `sidebar.lockAll`, `sidebar.hideAll`, `statusBar.clock`, `statusBar.scale`.
