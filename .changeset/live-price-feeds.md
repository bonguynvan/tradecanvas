'@tradecanvas/chart': minor

A price fed by hand is the live price. `updateLastBar`, `updateLastBarFromTick`, `appendBar` and `appendBars` now move the price line, the alerts and the orders' P&L, as a connected stream and `setCurrentPrice` do: alerts on a chart fed by your own code used to stay silent. A bar the chart can't take (a price that isn't a number, a high under its low) is left out, price and all. The first live price after `setData` crosses an alert's level from the last close (it only set the starting point), and a stream's bar close is a live price too. `dataUpdate` says what changed by `kind` (`'bars'` with the series' `length`, `'alert'`, `'bracket'`, `'connection'`, `'error'`), and every new bar sends it, by hand or by the stream; a tick on the forming bar doesn't. Its payload is typed as it is, `DataUpdatePayload` (exported): it was typed as a series it never carried, so code reading `e.payload.length` now checks `e.payload.kind === 'bars'` first. `getChartType()` reads back the chart type. `setTimezone('exchange')` shows the market's zone (`setMarket`) when the symbol names none, and follows a market set later; `getExchangeTimezone()` says which zone that is, and the widget offers it in its zone lists. The market presets and `setMarket` say what they hold and apply.
