The watchlist reads a line a symbol: the ticker, last price, move and % change, in columns under their names, about half as tall as before. The sparkline beside each price is off by default; `watchlist: { sparkline: true }` brings it back in the move's place. A ticker shows without its venue (`HYPERLIQUID:xyz:NVDA` reads NVDA, the whole symbol on hover).
