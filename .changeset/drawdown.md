`maxDrawdownPct` from `computeRiskMetrics` and the `Backtester` is now the deepest fall from a running peak. It was the percent of the largest fall in money, which on a growing account can be a later, shallower fall: an equity curve of 100, 50, 100, 400, 1000, 800, 1200 gave 0.2 (20%) instead of 0.5 (50%). It only ever gets larger, so a strategy filtered by a drawdown limit can now fail it. `calmar` divides by it and falls with it. `maxDrawdown` is still the largest fall in money, so the two can now come from different falls. The `RiskMetrics` fields say their units: the percentages are fractions (0.12 is 12%). `PerformanceDashboard` shows the total return and the max drawdown of a `Backtester` result in percent: they read 100 times too small, and for the curve above the Max Drawdown card showed `+0.20%`. It now reads as a fall, `-50.00%`, as the Drawdown panel under it plots it, whichever sign the drawdown is given with. Metrics you build by hand for it take fractions too (0.12 for 12%), as `RiskMetricsLike` now says: given in percent, as it used to show them, they now read 100 times too large. The `RiskMetrics` notes also give the units of `sharpe` and `sortino` (annualised ratios) and of `expectancy`, `averageWin` and `averageLoss` (money).
