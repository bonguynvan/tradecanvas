The widget's symbol search opens with names. `symbols` (the option and `setSymbols`) now takes `SymbolInfo` as well as tickers, and the list the search opens with shows each one's name, kind and exchange before anything is typed; it used to show bare tickers until a search had named them, so the description column stood empty. `SymbolInfo` has a new `logo` field, shown as a badge at the start of the row; a symbol without one gets the first letter of its ticker on a tint of its kind's colour (new `--tcw-kind-1` … `--tcw-kind-5` tokens, for both themes). A logo is only ever loaded as an image, with no referrer, and only from an `https:`/`http:` address, a path on your site, a `blob:` URL or a `data:image/…` one; anything else shows the letter. The toolbar's symbol and the symbol info panel take a name and an exchange from what you gave when the chart knows none, and no longer show the previous symbol's name when nothing replaced it; the chart's own info still comes first, and counts as the symbol's when it names it or was set since the symbol changed, so a feed's own spelling (`BTC-USD` for `BTCUSD`) still names it; a blank name or a numeric exchange code in it doesn't hide yours. In the search, the chart's info gives only a name or a logo your list lacks, so its own words for a kind or an exchange (`stock` where your list says `Cổ phiếu`, a broker's name) add no tab and no exchange to pick. A feed's search results win over your words, on the list the search opens with too once a search has told of a symbol, and keep your logo; a `null`, a number or a blank where a word goes (as a JSON feed may send) counts as not given, so it neither shows nor erases yours. Price steps and hours given in `symbols` are not applied to the chart.

The search looks different too: one grid, so the symbol, name, kind and exchange line up in every row under heads that stay at the top as the list scrolls, and the compare dialog's rows follow it. The kind tabs show as soon as the rows carry a kind, stay put while you type (a kind picked stays picked, and kinds that differ only in case are one), and when the rows come from two or more exchanges a list beside them keeps to one. These filters narrow the rows on hand (an adapter's search is asked for 50; a `searchSymbols` of yours gives what it gives); they do not search again. On the list before typing, a symbol you gave with no kind or exchange stays under a filter. With no search function, typing finds your symbols by name as well as ticker, with or without Vietnamese marks. On a phone the kind column gives way to the badge's colour, the tabs scroll in one line and fade at an end with more past it, and a venue before a colon (`BINANCE:BTCUSDT`) is dimmed and shortened first, so the ticker stays whole. A perpetual takes crypto's colour, apart from an exchange's futures, and the five colours are far enough apart to tell at a badge's size, from each other and from the accent and the up and down colours. The usual Vietnamese, Japanese, Chinese and Korean words for the kinds take the same colours as the English ones (`Cổ phiếu`, `株式`, `股票`, `주식` are all a stock's); a warrant takes the futures' colour, a fund or an ETF the index's, and a bond the forex's.

The list shows the best 200 rows, with a line under them saying how many more typing would narrow (the new `symbolSearch.more` message): every row shares its columns with the rest, so a list of a few thousand symbols took a tenth of a second or more on each keystroke. A logo that fails to load is not asked for again on the next keystroke. The exchange list keeps the keyboard as its arrows change it, where the search took it after the first. A kind or an exchange in Turkish capitals ("HİSSE") is one with its lower case ("Hisse"), and a kind named like a built-in of every object (`constructor`) gets a colour like any other. Right to left, a feed's kinds and exchanges read in their own direction on the tabs and in the exchange list ("Stock." no longer shows as ".Stock"). In the compare dialog, a kind or an exchange that only the chart's own symbol is of, which it never lists, gets no tab.

Passing objects in `symbols` from plain JavaScript no longer breaks the widget (the chart's symbol became an object, the watchlist came out empty, and typing a letter threw), nor does a name, kind or exchange that isn't text, in `symbols` or in a feed's results, nor a `"__proto__"` key in them, which is dropped. `symbols` entries are cleaned as a watchlist cleans them: trimmed, a repeat listed once, anything that isn't a ticker or a `SymbolInfo` with one dropped. `setSymbols` also replaces a watchlist made from `symbols` (no `lists`, nothing kept), as before; give the watchlist `lists` of its own when the symbols come from your server.
