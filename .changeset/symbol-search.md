The widget's symbol search opens with names. `symbols` (the option and `setSymbols`) now takes `SymbolInfo` as well as tickers, and the list the search opens with shows each one's name, kind and exchange before anything is typed; it used to show bare tickers until a search had named them, so the description column stood empty. `SymbolInfo` has a new `logo` field, shown as a badge at the start of the row; a symbol without one gets the first letter of its ticker on a tint of its kind's colour (new `--tcw-kind-1` … `--tcw-kind-5` tokens, for both themes). A logo is only ever loaded as an image, with no referrer, and only from an `https:`/`http:` address, a path on your site, a `blob:` URL or a `data:image/…` one; anything else shows the letter. The toolbar's symbol and the symbol info panel take a name and an exchange from what you gave when the chart knows none, and no longer show the previous symbol's name when nothing replaced it; the chart's own info still comes first. A feed's search results win over your words and keep your logo. Price steps and hours given in `symbols` are not applied to the chart.

The search looks different too: one grid, so the symbol, name, kind and exchange line up in every row under heads that stay at the top as the list scrolls, and the compare dialog's rows follow it. The kind tabs show as soon as the rows carry a kind, stay put while you type (a kind picked stays picked, and kinds that differ only in case are one), and when the rows come from two or more exchanges a list beside them keeps to one. These filters narrow the rows on hand, the feed's 50 at most; they do not search again. On the list before typing, a symbol you gave with no kind or exchange stays under a filter. With no search function, typing finds your symbols by name as well as ticker, with or without Vietnamese marks. On a phone the kind column gives way to the badge's colour and the tabs scroll in one line.

Passing objects in `symbols` from plain JavaScript no longer breaks the widget (the chart's symbol became an object, the watchlist came out empty, and typing a letter threw). `symbols` entries are cleaned as a watchlist cleans them: trimmed, a repeat listed once, anything that isn't a ticker or a `SymbolInfo` with one dropped. `setSymbols` also replaces a watchlist made from `symbols` (no `lists`, nothing kept), as before; give the watchlist `lists` of its own when the symbols come from your server.
