Three more drawing tools, 76 in all: `disjointChannel` (two lines leaning opposite ways), `flatTopBottom` (a sloping line and a level one) and `rotatedRectangle` (a rectangle at any angle). Each takes three points and a fill; the two channels extend and carry alerts.
