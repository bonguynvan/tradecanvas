The order ticket in the Print look. It names the symbol; the type and the time in force are choices in a row; − and + move the quantity by a lot and prices by the market's step, and a price from the chart starts rounded to that step (it was a raw float); a stop-loss or take-profit says how far it is in percent and what it would make or lose; a receipt sums up the order's value, risk, reward and reward to risk. Switching between buy and sell keeps a waiting order valid (a buy stop over the market becomes a sell limit), and Place takes the side's colour. The Trade menu sets the panels apart from the orders with a rule.
