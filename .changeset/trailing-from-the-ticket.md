An order can open a position that already trails: `trailingStop` on `OrderPlaceIntent` and `TradingOrder`, a Trailing stop switch under the stop-loss in the order ticket, and TSL on a waiting order's stop. The trail button on a stop's tag is filled while the stop is trailing.
