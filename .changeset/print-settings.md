The chart settings, rewritten. Tabs down the side, each with its icon: Symbol (the chart type's own settings and colours, the main series, the last price, the volume, the high and low), Status line, Scales, Appearance (background, grid, crosshair, session breaks, handles), Layers (the profiles, the depth heatmap, swings, sessions, prior levels) and Trading. Every change shows at once; Cancel, Escape, the close button or a click outside put back what the session changed, and OK keeps it as one undo step. The Template menu saves the look (the switches and the colours that are set; not the time zone, number format or auto-scale) under a name, applies or deletes a saved one, and resets to the defaults. A context menu's entries can now have a remove button (and the Delete key), and a long one scrolls. Fixed: a drag ending outside a dialog no longer closes it; a toast over an open dialog shows above it.
