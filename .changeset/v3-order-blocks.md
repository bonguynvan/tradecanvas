Order blocks: `addIndicator('orderblocks')` draws the bar the market last turned from before a close broke a swing, as a box to the right edge until a close goes through it; `showBroken: 1` keeps the broken ones. `detectOrderBlocks(bars, { length })` gives them with no chart.
