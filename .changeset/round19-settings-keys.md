The widget's Settings set every style key on the user's layer: the Style tab gives the chart type on view its own colours and widths, and covers the panes, legend, watermark, grid, crosshair, scales, last price, volume, session breaks and high and low lines; a new Trading tab sets the colours of orders, positions, signal markers and trade zones, shown as Auto until one is picked. Undo, Reset and the theme switch cover every key.
