Editing drawings feels like drawing them. The magnet pulls a dragged handle and a moved drawing onto a bar's prices too, and the weak magnet reaches 50 px. Hold Ctrl or ⌘ to turn the magnet over while drawing or dragging, and Shift to keep a line or a path flat, upright or at 45°; Shift + drag measures only on empty chart now. The arrow keys move the selected drawings a bar across or a pixel up or down (Shift: ten), one undo step for a run of presses. Ctrl/⌘ + drag on a drawing drags out a copy, one undo step; on a handle of the selected drawing it reshapes it with the magnet turned over. The shortcut sheet lists all four.
