Everything by finger: with a drawing tool, tap its points or press, drag and lift to draw a line; drawings are selected, moved and reshaped by touch, and a long press opens a drawing's menu. A tap is a click, a double tap a double-click (axis reset, drawing settings). Alert, order and bracket lines and pane dividers drag by finger, and a finger reaches further than a mouse (`TOUCH_REACH`). `pageScroll` (option and `setPageScroll`) leaves vertical swipes and the plain wheel to the page; the chart pans sideways and zooms with two fingers or Ctrl + wheel. A chart that neither pans nor zooms leaves the page's scrolling alone; `touchcancel` ends a gesture without a click.
