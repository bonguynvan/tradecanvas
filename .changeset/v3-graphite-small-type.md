In the Graphite look the smallest type (`--tcw-font-size-xs`) is the small size, 12 px, so nothing to read in the widget is 10 px; a look that sets `ui.font.smallSize` has nothing smaller. A new token, `--tcw-font-size-glyph`, keeps a character inside a set shape (a keycap, a badge's letter) two steps under the body. The watchlist's row for the symbol on the chart has no accent bar.
