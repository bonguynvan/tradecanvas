A new default look, Print: paper and ink, kept to the golden ratio. The chart's dark and light themes are warm ink on an off-black and on an off-white paper, with deeper candles, the line chart in ink, volume in one neutral ink and every figure in a monospaced face; the crosshair and the last-price line are solid hairlines. The widget's new `print` preset is the default (`ui: 'studio'` brings back the old one): hairlines, small corners, capital labels, a chosen button in solid ink and an ink-blue accent. Its sizes climb in half steps of φ (16, 20, 26, 33, 42), spacing and corners are Fibonacci numbers, a menu row's corner nests inside its menu's, and type climbs a third of φ at a time (10, 11, 13, 15, 18, 21) in every look. `PHI`, `FIBONACCI` and `phiStep` come from `@tradecanvas/commons`.
