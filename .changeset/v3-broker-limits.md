`chart.setPriceLimits({ ceiling, floor, reference })` takes the day's limits as the broker or the exchange gives them for the symbol, over what the market's percentages make of a reference. The widget's order ticket shows the ceiling, the reference and the floor over its price, each a press that sets the price, in the order `priceLimitOrder` gives.
