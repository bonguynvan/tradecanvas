'@tradecanvas/chart': patch
---

A selected drawing's toolbar waits in one place, near the top of the price pane (at its foot on a narrow chart), whichever pane the drawing is on, where it was put over the drawing and covered a text or a price label just drawn; it no longer follows the drawing. Dragging it by its grip keeps it where it is dropped, and a double-click on the grip sends it back. The widget's Vietnamese strings keep the trading terms Vietnamese traders use in English (Bar replay, Liquidity heatmap, Crosshair, Watchlist, Trend line, DOM ladder, Spread…).
