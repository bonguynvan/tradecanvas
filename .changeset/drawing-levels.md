Lines, channels, Fibonacci and Gann tools read cleaner: each Fibonacci ratio drawn with others has a colour of its own, bands are shaded alike, percentages are written whole (50%), and a label that would cover another is left out (never the 0 and 1 levels). Fibonacci time zones start from their 0 line, and their and cyclic lines' handles sit by the plot's foot. Gann boxes label their divisions in price and in time, Gann fans every angle where it leaves the plot, and grids and guides are sharp 1 px lines. A speed resistance fan's rays take their levels' colours. A parallel channel is its base line moved straight up or down to its third point, as its alerts already read it; a regression channel is the least-squares line through the closes between its points with bands two standard deviations either side, its handles on the line.
