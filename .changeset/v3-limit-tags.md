A day's limit tags on the price axis are the height and type of the tags beside them and are filled so that their figure reads at 4.5:1. A new style key, `lastPrice.limitColors` (off by default), draws the last price's line and tag in a limit's colour while the price is at the ceiling, the floor or the reference price. The chart writes nothing under 10 px.
