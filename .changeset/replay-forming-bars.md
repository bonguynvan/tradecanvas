Bars form as a replay plays. With `animate: true` (`replayStart`, `ReplayConfig`) each bar grows over its time as a live one does, from its open to the extreme its direction says came first, the other one, then its close, and ends as the bar is in the series; a pause holds it half drawn, a seek or a step shows whole bars, and a paper account fills as the drawn price reaches an order. `step: 'auto'` (or an interval such as `'1m'`) shapes each bar with finer bars the chart fetches through the connected feed's `fetchHistoryBefore` for the bars it is about to play, only while it plays, one request at a time; `'auto'` is the finest the feed serves with at most 60 to a bar, and the new `replayStepsLoad` event says how the fetching goes. Finer `steps` you pass shape the bars the same way with `animate`; without it they stay steps of their own, as before, now keeping only the hours shown, and a bar's last step shows the bar as the series has it. Indices, `speed` and `duration` count the chart's bars, and `getReplayBarIndex()` is the bar forming. Bars shorter than three frames come whole; under reduced motion a bar steps between its prices. `ReplayManager` gains a `phase` event, `getFraction()` and `getStepMs()`. ChartWidget replays this way by default: the Step menu starts on Auto, the new `replayStep` and `replayAnimate` options set it, step forward completes a half-drawn bar first, and the widget no longer fetches the newest finer bars, which were not the ones replayed, when the feed has `fetchHistoryBefore`; with `replayAnimate: false`, Auto replays whole bars as before; on a feed without it the widget fetches them in one request as before and the bars form along them.
