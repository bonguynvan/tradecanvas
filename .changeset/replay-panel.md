ChartWidget's replay bar is redesigned. It is a compact bar on the foot of the main pane, centred on its plot, so bottom panes stay readable; toasts and the phone's drawing button move above it. It shows the date and time of the step on screen (in the display timezone, the year only when it isn't this one) and fills the played part of its track. Speeds are multipliers from 0.1× to 50×, where 1× plays one bar of the chart a second whatever the step, and the default is now 1× (it was 5 bars a second); with `replayAnimate: false` and finer steps a speed now means chart bars, not steps, a second. **Select bar** pauses and picks another start among the bars shown without showing the bars to come, and Cancel or Esc goes back to the same place. Space on the chart or the track plays or pauses during a replay (outside one it still fits the chart), and Shift+←/→ now step only the widget used last, never the replay behind an open dialog, after a shortcut you added with `addHotkey` on the same keys. The toolbar's Bar replay button shows as pressed while the replay is open, focus goes back to it when the bar closes, the keyboard-shortcut sheet lists the replay's keys (and Space, not F, for fitting the chart), and the bar opens without jumping sideways. On a phone or a narrow chart it takes two rows. In a right-to-left widget the bar runs left to right with time, its words read right to left, and a select's chevron no longer sits over its value. `replayStep` gains `stepTime`, when the step shown opened, and the icon set gains `replayPick`. Nine new strings in all 30 languages (`replay.selectBar`, `replay.rate`, `replay.rateHint`, `replay.playingAt`, `replay.pausedAt`, `replay.endedAt`, `hotkeys.replayStep`, `hotkeys.replayPlay`, `hotkeys.replayCancel`); `replay.barsPerSecond` is no longer used. Hosts that styled `.tcw-replay-progress` lose it: the bar shows the date instead.
