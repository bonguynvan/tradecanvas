A calmer chart. The candles keep clear of the volume band (φ^-4.5 of the plot, with a gap of φ^-7), and the scale leaves φ^-5 above and below the prices. Tick labels show only the decimals their step needs (73,500). Order, position and alert prices are written as the axis writes them, grouped in the number locale, with an order's quantity on its line rather than its axis tag. Indicator value tags are off by default. The bar countdown hangs under the last-price tag. The crosshair's pills clear the axis labels they would half cover. The legend opens with a title line (symbol · interval · exchange) that carries the prices and wraps on a narrow plot. The zoom and scroll buttons show only near the plot's bottom. New in commons: `fitPriceRange` and `priceTagText`.
