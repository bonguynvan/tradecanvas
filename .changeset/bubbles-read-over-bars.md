A note, a callout, a signpost and a balloon write in a bubble filled with their colour, in the ink that reads on it, where a faint tint let the candles show through their words; a fill or a text colour set by hand is kept. A callout's pointer is its bubble's tail, leaving the side that faces the point, in place of a hairline. The box a text is typed in is as wide as the text on the chart, so a line no longer breaks in two while it is typed, and an empty one shows the tool's name.
