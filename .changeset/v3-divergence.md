Divergences: `addIndicator('rsidiv')` is the RSI with a line between its two turns wherever the price went on and the RSI did not, and a mark at the second; `hidden: 1` adds the hidden ones. `detectDivergences(bars, line, options)` reads any line with one value a bar.
