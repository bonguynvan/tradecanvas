Toolbar buttons and menus you add with `addToolbarButton` or `addToolbarDropdown` and `side: 'left'` now fold under the toolbar's "more" menu like the widget's own, after everything on the right. Before, they never folded: on a phone they pushed "more" off the edge of the screen, and everything under it with it. This changes what a narrow widget (under 641 px) shows: a left button of yours that used to stay on the bar there now goes under "more" with the rest, listed first. A menu of yours picked from "more" opens under "more" and, like the "more" menu, ends where "more" ends; it used to open at the widget's top-left corner. Pressing "more" again closes it, as it closes the Trade and Layouts menus picked from it, so two menus are never open at once. The toolbar refits when a button of yours changes width, so a count that grows (`handle.setText`) folds buttons instead of pushing "more" out of the widget, and a count as wide as the last costs no layout: a live count set every frame is free. A count passed as a number from plain JavaScript shows as text, and `null` or `undefined` clears it, as before. In "more", a button of yours is named with its count ("News: 3"), and a switch (yours with `toggle`, the price alerts, the object tree) is listed as a switch whether it is on or off, with its icon while off; your menu entries with `checked: false` show their `icon` too, where they showed none. When a refit folds the button the keyboard is on, the keyboard moves to "more", and when "more" goes, to the last button that came back, never to the page; a menu hanging from "more", or from a button that folded, closes. An `onClick` of yours still gets its own button when it is picked from "more"; that button is folded and has no size then, so a popover you place from it lands at the corner. Place it from "more" when `button.getClientRects()` is empty: `button.closest('.tcw-toolbar')?.querySelector('.tcw-toolbar-more')`, as the note on `onClick` says.
