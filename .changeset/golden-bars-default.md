The widget opens the chart with more bars on screen: golden bars, the body φ⁻¹ of its slot at every zoom, opening at φ⁴ ≈ 6.85 px a slot. That is about 250 bars across a 1920 px screen, from about 170. `ui: { density: 'compact' }` keeps the classic bars, a 2 px gap in a 10 px slot, and `chartOptions.barLayout` still wins over the look. A `Chart` of your own keeps `'classic'` as its default.
