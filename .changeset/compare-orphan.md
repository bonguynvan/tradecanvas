'@tradecanvas/chart': patch
---

**A compare whose bars don't load no longer leaves an empty indicator behind.** `widget.addCompareSymbol(symbol, way)` added a compare on its own scale or pane, a spread, a ratio or a correlation before it knew whether your adapter had the other symbol's bars. When the fetch failed, or came back empty at the chart's interval, an empty 'Compare Symbol' stayed on the chart and in its saves, and came back after every reload. The widget now fetches the bars first in every way, as it already did for the percent way: when they don't come it shows the error in a toast and adds nothing; when they do, the chart gets them before the indicator, so they are fetched once, not twice. An empty answer no longer adds an empty percent line either. A compare that a saved state brings back stays on the chart when its bars fail to load, and fills on an interval that has them. The Objects panel names each indicator as its chip and the legend do ('Compare ETHUSDT', 'EMA 20'), and its Compare section lists every compare with its way, as the compare dialog does; its remove button takes off a compare that is an indicator too.
