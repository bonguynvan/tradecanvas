A filled tag on the price axis (the last price, a quote, an indicator's value) is filled a shade deeper where its ink would not read on it at 4.5:1. In the themes that outline the values on their axis (`Theme.valueTagsOutlined`, the Graphite themes), the bid, ask, high and low tags are outlined too. The chart's smaller type (limit tags, pane titles, alert and signal labels, the measure tool) follows the theme's small size, and the crosshair's percentage is no longer written with the last price switched off.
