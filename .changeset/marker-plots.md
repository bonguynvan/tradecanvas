A plot of `kind: 'markers'` marks the bars where its field has a value: a `shape` (`'dot'`, `'triangleUp'`, `'triangleDown'`) at the value, or above the bar's high or below its low on the price pane (`position: 'aboveBar' | 'belowBar'`). It gets no value in the legend, no tag on the price axis, no column in the CSV export and no place in the data window, and is not offered as a line another indicator can read.
