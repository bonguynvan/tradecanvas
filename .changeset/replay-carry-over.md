A replay goes on when the bars under it change. Started while another timeframe's history loaded, it replayed the old bars and kept the new ones out of sight until it stopped; a symbol or timeframe switch during a replay did the same, `setData` ended it, and older bars were dropped. Now the replay is a time over the live series: a new symbol or timeframe (`connect`, `switchStream`), `setData`, older bars (`prependBars` returns their count, and history pages in during a replay), the extended hours and a corrected bar carry it over to the same time, showing only bars that closed by then. While a new series loads it waits, playing or paused as it was, and says nothing on `replayState`; a `duration` keeps the time it had left. A replay a paper account trades ends when the symbol changes. `replaySeekToTime` no longer shows a coarser bar whole before it closes, so a grid's synced replay doesn't give the coming hours away. `replayStep` gains `cause` (`'play'`, `'seek'` or `'series'`), `getReplayProgress()` gains `time` and `until`, and a page of history dropped by a new series is reported as `loaded` with no bars. Indices (`barIndex`, `total`, `replaySeekToBar`) are of the bars replayed now; keep a position by time. To end a replay on a new series as before, call `replayStop()` first. In ChartWidget the replay bar stays open across a switch, says Loading chart and waits while the bars load, then follows their total and the new interval's steps.
