A price set by hand on the chart keeps to the market's grid: lines dragged (orders, stops, targets, levels), a bracket's and a draft order's lines, a stop added from its button, an alert dragged, the right-click menu's orders, and the prices of `priceAxisAdd` and `chartContextMenu`. They are on the market's price step, else to the decimals the price axis shows (`chart.roundPrice`), and the crosshair writes the price under the pointer on the same grid.
