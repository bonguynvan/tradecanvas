The ink on a filled tag of the chart (the last price, an indicator's value, a mark) is chosen by contrast with its fill, in every theme: dark ink on the candles' green and red, where white was under 3:1. In the Graphite themes an indicator's value tags on the price axis are outlined, so the last price is the one block of colour there (`Theme.valueTagsOutlined`; the `axis.price.valueTagsOutlined` style key is over it), and the chart's small type is 12 px, as the legend's prices are.
