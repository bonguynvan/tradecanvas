Symbol search, Compare and Go to, rebuilt. Symbol search is a dialog with the results in columns (the symbol, its name, its kind and exchange), chips to narrow results of more than one kind to one, and the names a search told of kept for next time; a letter typed on the chart opens it with that letter (`features: { symbolTyping: false }` leaves letters alone). Compare opens the same dialog with **Add as** under the search (percent, own scale, own pane, spread, ratio, correlation of prices or of returns), stays open for the next symbol, lists what is compared already with a button to take each off, and opens next time on the way used last; the menu of ways after a pick is gone. Go to is a dialog with two tabs: Date centres a day and a time, Custom range shows the bars between two of them edge to edge, the month under the fields filling the date in hand; no time on daily bars, no days after today (today in the chart's time zone). New `chart.getVisibleTimeRange()`: the first and last bars on screen. A press elsewhere on the page now leaves the keyboard to the page until the widget is pressed again. On a phone, dialogs keep their top on the screen above the keyboard.
