A heatmap's cell may carry a `logo`, drawn over its name where the cell has room: the addresses a symbol's `logo` takes, loaded once, left out when it fails or when its site does not allow it to be read (CORS), so the chart can still be saved as a picture. `showLogos: false` draws none.
