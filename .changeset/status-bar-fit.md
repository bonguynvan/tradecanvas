The widget's status bar fits its width and keeps the clock whole. On a phone, or in a narrow column or a grid's cell, the clock used to be cut off at the bar's edge (on a 360 px phone with a Vietnamese market, only its hour showed), the range presets past the room hid in a scroller with no scrollbar ("All", "5Y" and go to date out of sight), and your items from `addStatusBarItem` on the left and the right could run into each other ("Dem42 ms"). Now the right group (the market's status, the connection, the clock, your items there) keeps its width, and when the range bar has too little room its presets fold under a "more" button, the longest range first, then go to date. "More" shows only while something is folded, and opens a menu of what is: the presets, then go to date with its Alt+G. Only when all of that is folded and the bar still has no room does the market's status shorten to a word ("Market closed" becomes "Closed"; the full words stay in its tooltip and for screen readers). Your items on the left keep their width too, with a gap before the right group, so the presets fold sooner for wide ones. Switching off `statusBar.range` hides "more" with the presets, and go to date then never folds; with `statusBar.goToDate` off it stays out of the menu. Right to left, the range bar keeps its gap before the right group, where it used to overlap it by 8 px. The presets close up below 641 px of the widget's own width, as the rest of its phone layout does, where they followed the window's width.
