The widget's symbol search shows a symbol's last price and day's change when you give them: `SymbolInfo` takes `last` (a number, or text written as it is) and `changePercent`, and when any row listed has one they show in two columns at the end of it, the change in the up or down colour. The widget fetches none of them. On a phone the exchange goes under the ticker, where a long one (SIMULATED, ALL VENUES) was cut short in a column of its own.
