# Soak, 2026-11-03

The widget left running on a busy feed: does it hold its frame rate, and do
its memory, DOM nodes or event listeners creep up? `pnpm build && pnpm soak`
runs it (`scripts/soak.mjs`, the page in `scripts/soak`).

The desk: the full `ChartWidget` on 1,000,000 one-second bars, drawn with
WebGL at 1600×900 and a pixel ratio of 1; EMA 21, Bollinger Bands 20, RSI 14
and MACD; a paper broker with a position, its stop and target, and three
working orders that are filled, placed again and moved back near the price
every 15 seconds, with the oldest position closed past two; the bid and ask;
a depth heatmap fed four times a second; and a worker that stands in for the
socket, sending 30,000 trades a second in a message every 4 ms. Every trade
reaches the chart as a tick (`connect({ aggregateTicks: true })`). Every 30
seconds the runner collects garbage and reads the page's metrics.

Headless Chrome on an Intel UHD 770 (Core i7-14700, 32 GB), Windows 11.

## Before and after

`main` at 8a8a571 (1.68.0), then with the three changes below.

| | 1.68.0 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Frames a second | 25.6–34.2 | 56.3–56.9 |
| p95 frame | 52.8–87.9 ms | 18.1–18.3 ms |
| Main-thread tasks over 50 ms | 14–21 every 15 s | 0 |
| Trades taken in, a second | 30,000 | 30,000 |

A CPU profile of ten seconds after the warm-up (`pnpm soak --profile=desk.cpuprofile`):

- **1.68.0:** 66% of the main thread in `TimeMarker.weigh`, which checked every bar's time against its cache on every frame, twice a frame, on a million bars. Next came the day and month lines (`SessionBreaks.computeBreaksTyped`, 4%): they went over the whole history again for each new bar, about 46 ms once a second. Then the depth heatmap (5%), which sorted each of 240 snapshots' prices on every frame. Taking the 30,000 trades in cost 1.3%.
- **Now:** 70% of the main thread idle. Drawing is most of the rest; the trades are under 1%.

## The changes

- `TimeMarker` (`packages/core/src/axis/timeMarks.ts`): the same array, grown
  in place, keeps its weights after a look at its last weighed bar only; the
  weights grow in place with room to spare; the marks are picked by a counting
  sort over the weights rather than a pass over every slot for each level.
- `SessionBreaks` (`packages/core/src/ui/SessionBreaks.ts`): a series that
  still has its first time and its last looked-at time is looked at from
  there on.
- `DepthHeatmapBuffer` (`packages/core/src/charts`): a snapshot's level
  spacing is measured when it is pushed, not on each frame it is drawn.
