# Code of Conduct

TradeCanvas follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, to newcomers most of all.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Assume good faith, and accept a maintainer's decision on what belongs in the project.

This applies in issues, pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, tell the maintainer, [@bonguynvan](https://github.com/bonguynvan). To report it privately, open an issue titled "Code of conduct" with no details, and the maintainer will get in touch with you away from the public thread. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, or block people who don't follow this code, temporarily or for good.
